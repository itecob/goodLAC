# goodLAC Contributor License Agreement — DRAFT

> **DRAFT — LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED BEFORE ACTIVATION**
>
> **THIS DRAFT IS NOT AN ACTIVE CONTRIBUTOR AGREEMENT.**
>
> It records the intended contributor-rights model for legal review. Do not require contributors to accept this draft, do not represent it as legally approved, and do not enable an automated CLA bot against it.

## 1. Parties and contribution

This draft contemplates an agreement between **ITECOB Inc.** ("ITECOB") and the individual or entity making a contribution ("Contributor") to goodLAC.

"Contribution" is intended to include copyrightable code, documentation, tests, designs, or other material intentionally submitted by Contributor for inclusion in goodLAC, subject to exclusions and mechanics to be finalized by legal review.

## 2. Contributor ownership

Contributor retains copyright ownership of the Contribution except for rights owned by another party and properly disclosed by Contributor.

Nothing in the intended agreement requires assignment of Contributor's copyright to ITECOB.

## 3. Authority to contribute

Contributor is intended to represent that:

- Contributor has sufficient rights and authority to submit the Contribution and grant the rights described by the agreement;
- the Contribution does not knowingly violate an obligation to an employer, client, or other third party;
- any third-party code, data, content, license condition, or other restriction included in or required by the Contribution is clearly disclosed; and
- where Contributor submits on behalf of an entity, Contributor is authorized to bind or act for that entity as required by the final agreement.

## 4. Copyright license to ITECOB

The intended agreement grants ITECOB a **perpetual, worldwide, irrevocable, royalty-free, non-exclusive** license, with rights to sublicense and relicense, to:

- use;
- reproduce;
- modify;
- prepare derivative works of;
- publicly display and perform where applicable;
- distribute;
- sublicense;
- license; and
- otherwise exploit

the Contribution as part of or in connection with goodLAC and related products, services, documentation, distributions, and derivative works.

The intended grant must be broad enough for ITECOB to distribute accepted Contributions under:

1. the public goodLAC Community License or any later public license selected by ITECOB; and
2. separate commercial, proprietary, negotiated, or other licenses.

Legal review must ensure that the final language accomplishes this without creating ambiguity.

## 5. Patent rights

The intended contributor framework must address patent rights, where applicable, sufficiently to prevent an accepted Contribution from blocking ordinary use, distribution, sublicensing, or commercial relicensing of goodLAC.

**LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED:** the precise patent-license grant, scope, defensive termination terms, entity coverage, and any exclusions must be drafted or approved by qualified counsel before activation.

## 6. Third-party material

Contributor must identify any third-party material or restriction associated with a Contribution.

ITECOB may reject a Contribution if its license, provenance, patent position, confidentiality obligation, or other restriction is incompatible with goodLAC's public and commercial distribution model.

## 7. No obligation to accept or maintain

Submission does not obligate ITECOB to:

- accept or merge the Contribution;
- distribute the Contribution;
- continue using the Contribution;
- maintain any feature;
- provide support; or
- compensate Contributor.

## 8. No automatic endorsement or employment relationship

A Contribution is not intended to create employment, partnership, agency, certification, endorsement, or other relationship beyond the final contributor agreement.

## 9. Agreement mechanics

**LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED** for:

- the mechanism by which an individual or entity accepts the final agreement;
- records of acceptance;
- treatment of pre-activation contributions;
- governing law and venue;
- representations, disclaimers, and limitation language;
- patent terms;
- entity-contributor authority;
- privacy/data handling for contributor records; and
- amendment/versioning procedures.

## 10. Activation

This draft must not be activated until legal review is complete and ITECOB Inc. expressly approves the final text.

No CLA bot or automated acceptance workflow should be enabled against this draft.
