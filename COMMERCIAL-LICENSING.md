# goodLAC Commercial Licensing Policy

> **STATUS: INTENDED POLICY — LEGAL REVIEW AND LICENSE ACTIVATION REQUIRED**

This document records ITECOB Inc.'s approved business-policy intent for goodLAC. It is not an operative software license and does not itself grant permission. The repository currently has no activated project license; `LICENSE-DRAFT.md` remains a legal-review draft.

## Intended positioning

Once an operative Community License is activated:

> The goodLAC core is intended to be provided as source-available software so individuals and organizations can understand, inspect, modify, and build their own agent systems around a transparent authority boundary. The complete functionality represented as part of this repository will be governed by its public license. Other products, modules, services, or applications developed by ITECOB Inc. may be released under separate source-available, commercial, or other licenses.

> **goodLAC is intended to be free for personal, research, educational, and internal organizational use. Commercial licensing is intended to be required when goodLAC is incorporated into, resold as, or used to provide a commercial product, SaaS, hosted service, or managed service to third parties.**

## Controlling distinction

The intended licensing boundary is based on **use**, not entity type, revenue, organization size, or charitable status.

The primary question is:

> **Whose systems, accounts, resources, users, or service functionality is goodLAC operating?**

Use by a person or organization to operate its **own** systems and workflows is intended to be internal/self use. This remains true when goodLAC helps that organization produce work that is later delivered to paying customers.

Use of goodLAC itself to control, administer, authorize, operate, host, or provide functionality for **third-party** systems, accounts, resources, users, or customers is intended to require a commercial license.

## Intended free/internal use

No commercial goodLAC license is intended to be required for:

- personal use;
- research;
- education;
- self-hosting for the user's own personal or internal organizational use;
- internal organizational use;
- internal business administration;
- employees using goodLAC to operate their own organization;
- internal email, calendar, marketing, development, finance, operations, HR, research, or other workflows;
- internal use that helps create work ultimately delivered to paying customers;
- internal use by a company or organization of any size;
- ordinary unpaid personal assistance between individuals;
- permitted noncommercial forks and redistribution under the Community License conditions; and
- paid installation, setup, configuration, training, auditing, migration, or support when goodLAC is deployed for the customer's own internal use and the provider does not continue operating goodLAC on the customer's behalf.

### Examples

A plumber using goodLAC to send the plumber's own marketing email is intended to be **free internal use**.

An accountant using goodLAC internally to help prepare a client's report is intended to be **free internal use**.

A large corporation using goodLAC internally for employee workflows is intended to be **free internal use**.

A consultant charging to install goodLAC on a customer's server, configuring it, handing control to the customer, and not continuing to operate goodLAC on the customer's behalf is intended to be **permitted without a commercial productization license**.

A marketing agency using goodLAC to run the agency's own internal business is intended to be **free internal use**.

## Intended commercial-license boundary

A commercial agreement with ITECOB Inc. is intended to be required when goodLAC itself becomes operational functionality provided to third parties.

This includes, without limitation:

- embedding goodLAC into commercial software;
- embedding goodLAC into a paid product;
- SaaS incorporating goodLAC;
- hosted goodLAC services;
- managed goodLAC services;
- OEM or white-label offerings;
- selling or licensing goodLAC itself;
- using goodLAC as part of software sold or licensed to customers;
- operating goodLAC on behalf of customers;
- using goodLAC to control, administer, authorize, or operate customer systems or accounts as an ongoing service;
- agencies or service providers using goodLAC operationally to perform managed work inside client systems; and
- commercial offerings in which goodLAC supplies part of the functionality being sold or operated for the customer.

### Examples

A marketing agency using goodLAC operationally to manage client accounts or campaigns is intended to require a **commercial license**.

An AI SaaS platform using goodLAC as part of the authority/effect-control layer delivered to its customers is intended to require a **commercial license**.

A managed-service provider retaining operational control of a customer's goodLAC deployment is intended to require a **commercial license**.

## Entity type is not controlling

The intended policy does not turn on whether the user is:

- an individual or corporation;
- for-profit or nonprofit;
- charitable or non-charitable; or
- small or large.

Internal/self use is intended to remain free. Third-party operational use and productization are intended to require commercial licensing.

Ordinary unpaid personal assistance between individuals is intended to remain permitted.

## Paid installation and support

The intended model permits third parties to charge for:

- installation;
- configuration;
- training;
- migration;
- auditing; and
- support

when the customer's own internal deployment is handed to and operated by the customer.

If the provider continues to operate goodLAC on the customer's behalf, that use is intended to require a commercial license.

Third-party installation, configuration, support, or auditing does not constitute certification, verification, endorsement, partnership, or approval by ITECOB Inc.

There is currently no qualified `goodLAC verify-install` feature or installation-certification program.

## Commercial agreements

Commercial licensing is intended to be available only by individual agreement with ITECOB Inc.

Commercial licenses are intended to be discretionary. ITECOB Inc. is not intended to be obligated to grant a commercial license and may approve, decline, limit, or condition commercial use.

ITECOB Inc. may agree to commercial terms for a fee, for no fee, or under other negotiated terms.

There is no automatic commercial-license entitlement, fixed public pricing, automatic licensing tier, or promise that every applicant will receive a commercial license.

**Owner action required:** add the approved commercial-licensing contact channel before activation.

## No automatic relicensing

No goodLAC version is intended to become commercially unrestricted merely because of age.

ITECOB Inc. may voluntarily relicense a version in the future, but no automatic conversion to another license is promised.

## Relationship to the operative license

When a reviewed Community License is formally activated, the operative license text controls legal permissions and restrictions. This policy should then be checked against that license for consistency.

Until that occurs, this file records intended business policy only and is not a substitute for an operative license or legal advice.
