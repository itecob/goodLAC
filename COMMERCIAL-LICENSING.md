# goodLAC Commercial Licensing Policy

> **STATUS: APPROVED BUSINESS POLICY — COMMERCIAL AGREEMENTS NEGOTIATED SEPARATELY**

This document records ITECOB Inc.'s approved business-policy boundary for goodLAC. It is not itself a commercial licence and does not grant commercial permission.

## Positioning

Under the intended goodLAC Community License, goodLAC is free for personal, research, educational, and internal organizational use.

A separate commercial licence is required when goodLAC itself becomes functionality sold, licensed, hosted, managed, operated, or otherwise provided to third parties.

## Controlling distinction

The licensing boundary is based primarily on **use**, not entity type, revenue, organization size, or charitable status.

The primary question is:

> **Whose systems, accounts, resources, users, or service functionality is goodLAC operating?**

Use by a person or organization to operate its **own** systems and workflows is Internal Use. This remains true when goodLAC helps that organization produce work or deliverables that are later provided to paying customers, provided goodLAC itself is not being supplied or operated as functionality for those customers.

Use of goodLAC itself to control, administer, authorize, operate, host, manage, or provide functionality for **third-party** systems, accounts, resources, users, or customers requires a separate commercial licence.

## Free/internal use

No commercial goodLAC licence is intended to be required for:

- personal use;
- research;
- education;
- self-hosting for the user's own personal or internal organizational use;
- internal organizational use;
- internal business administration;
- employees using goodLAC to operate their own organization;
- internal email, calendar, marketing, development, finance, operations, HR, research, or similar workflows;
- internal use that helps create work ultimately delivered to paying customers;
- internal use by a company or organization of any size;
- ordinary unpaid personal assistance between individuals;
- permitted noncommercial forks and redistribution under the Community License; and
- paid installation, setup, configuration, training, auditing, migration, or support where goodLAC is deployed for the customer's own internal use and goodLAC itself is not sold or operated as ongoing third-party functionality.

### Examples

A plumber using goodLAC to send the plumber's own marketing email is free Internal Use.

An accountant using goodLAC internally to help prepare a client's report is free Internal Use.

A large corporation using goodLAC internally for employee workflows is free Internal Use.

A consultant charging to install and configure goodLAC for a customer's own internal deployment is permitted professional service where the consultant is not selling goodLAC itself or turning it into ongoing third-party operational functionality.

A marketing agency using goodLAC to run the agency's own internal business is free Internal Use.

## Uses requiring a commercial licence

A separate commercial agreement with ITECOB Inc. is required for, without limitation:

- selling or licensing goodLAC itself;
- selling or licensing a modified goodLAC distribution;
- charging for access to goodLAC functionality;
- embedding goodLAC into commercial software or a paid product;
- SaaS incorporating goodLAC;
- hosted goodLAC services;
- managed goodLAC services;
- OEM or white-label offerings;
- using goodLAC as part of software sold or licensed to customers;
- operating goodLAC on behalf of customers;
- using goodLAC to control, administer, authorize, or operate customer systems or accounts as ongoing functionality;
- agencies or service providers using goodLAC operationally as part of a managed service inside client systems; and
- commercial offerings in which goodLAC supplies functionality being sold or operated for a third party.

### Examples

A marketing agency using goodLAC operationally to manage client accounts or campaigns as a managed service requires a commercial licence.

An AI SaaS platform using goodLAC as part of the authority/effect-control layer delivered to customers requires a commercial licence.

A managed-service provider retaining operational control of customer goodLAC deployments requires a commercial licence.

A person who forks goodLAC, modifies it, and makes the fork available for free may do so subject to the Community License conditions. Charging for that fork requires a commercial licence.

## Paid installation and support

The Community License is intended to permit third parties to charge for professional services such as:

- installation;
- configuration;
- training;
- migration;
- auditing; and
- support

where goodLAC is deployed for the customer's own Internal Use and the fee is for those professional services rather than for goodLAC itself.

Third-party installation, configuration, support, or auditing does not constitute certification, verification, endorsement, partnership, or approval by ITECOB Inc.

## Commercial enquiries

Commercial licences are available only by separate individual agreement with ITECOB Inc.

There is:

- no automatic entitlement to a commercial licence;
- no standard public commercial licence at this time;
- no fixed public pricing;
- no automatic tier;
- no promise to approve every application; and
- no launch-time policy governing renewal, amendment, warranty, indemnity, liability, support, or other terms of a future negotiated commercial agreement.

Those terms, if any, will be negotiated separately.

For commercial licensing enquiries, contact:

**support@itecob.com**

## No automatic relicensing

No goodLAC version becomes commercially unrestricted merely because of age.

ITECOB Inc. may voluntarily change the licensing of a version later, but no automatic conversion is promised.

## Relationship to the Community License

When the goodLAC Community License is activated, that operative licence controls the public permissions and restrictions it grants.

This policy explains the intended commercial boundary and enquiry process. A separate signed commercial agreement may grant different rights to a particular licensee.
